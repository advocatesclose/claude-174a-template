## 1. Engagement

You have retained Advocates Close Corp. ("the Firm") to represent you in connection with the matters described below. This letter confirms the terms of our engagement.

The Firm's principal is Javaad Ali, Esq. Your engagement is with the Firm. Mr. Ali will supervise all work performed on your behalf.

## 2. Scope of Representation

The Firm is engaged to represent you in connection with:

(a) The documentation, substantiation, and defense of qualified research expenditure under IRC Section 174A(d)(3) (as enacted by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, July 2025) arising from the development of software in the repository identified in the Scope directory of this engagement;

(b) Any examination, audit, or inquiry by the Internal Revenue Service, any state taxing authority, or any auditor, underwriter, or other third party concerning the characterization of the Client's development time, the role of AI tools in the development process, or the deductibility of amounts paid or incurred in connection with the development of software;

(c) Any dispute, claim, or proceeding — whether in anticipation of litigation or otherwise — between the Client and any tax authority, auditor, lender, investor, acquirer, shareholder, partner, limited partner, or other person, arising from or relating to the matters described above;

(d) Any dispute, claim, or proceeding between the Client and any AI tool provider, technology company, or other third party concerning the ownership, attribution, or characterization of work product created during the development of the software identified in the Scope directory;

(e) Such other matters as may arise in connection with the foregoing, without limitation and whether or not in connection with the original intention of this representation.

## 3. Fee

The fee for this engagement is **$199.99**, due upon execution of this letter. This fee establishes the attorney-client relationship and covers the initial setup of the documentation framework described herein. Additional work, if requested, will be billed at the Firm's standard rates.

## 4. Attorney-Client Privilege

All communications between you and the Firm, including all materials in this repository's `.claude/` directory, are made in the course of and in connection with this legal representation. These communications are protected by the attorney-client privilege and, to the extent applicable, the work product doctrine.

This includes, without limitation:
- All transcripts of development sessions (`.claude/Transcripts/`)
- All logs of AI tool reasoning and decision processes (`.claude/Logs/`)
- All timesheets and time records (`.claude/Timesheets/`)
- All guidance and standing instructions (`.claude/Guidance/`)
- All internal memoranda and supporting materials (`.claude/Internal/`)
- All materials in the Scope, Onboarding, and Engagement directories

The privilege belongs to you, the Client. Only you may waive it.

## 5. AI Tool Identification

As part of this engagement, you are required to identify all AI tools actually used in the development of the repository that is the subject of this engagement. See `Scope/ai-tool-registry.md`. This registry serves two purposes:

(a) **Attribution clarity.** It establishes which AI tools were actually used, by whom, and when. This forecloses claims by third parties — including AI tool providers, competitors, or acquirers — that some other tool, suggestion, comment, email, or conversation was the source of the Client's work product.

(b) **Conflict identification.** It identifies the AI tool providers whose interests may be adverse to the Client's in foreseeable disputes. See `ConflictWaivers/`.

## 6. Conflict Waivers

The Client acknowledges that the Firm does not represent and will not represent the Client in any matter brought by or against the AI tool providers identified in `ConflictWaivers/ai-provider-waivers.md`. The Firm expressly declines any obligation to represent the Client in disputes with these entities. The Client waives any conflict arising from this limitation.

The Firm expresses no view regarding the division of any proceeds, gains, or other spoils as among the Client and any other party. The Firm's duty runs with the Client's interest in the spoils and not with any individual co-venturer, co-investor, or other victor.

## 7. No Guarantee of Outcome

The Firm makes no guarantee regarding the outcome of any examination, audit, dispute, or proceeding. The Firm will use its best professional judgment to protect your interests.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this engagement at any time upon written notice. The attorney-client privilege survives termination.
