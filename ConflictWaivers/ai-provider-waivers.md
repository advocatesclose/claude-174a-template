# Conflict Waivers — AI Tool Providers

## Purpose

The Client acknowledges that certain AI tool providers have interests that are or may become adverse to the Client's. The Firm expressly declines to represent the Client in any matter brought by or against these entities. The Client waives any conflict arising from this limitation.

This waiver is necessitated by the nature of the representation. The Firm represents multiple clients who use AI tools in software development. The AI tool providers identified below are parties whose technology is known to the Firm to have been actually used in the development of repositories subject to the Firm's engagements. Representing the Client against these entities — or these entities against the Client — would create an impermissible conflict.

The Firm expresses no view regarding the division of any proceeds, gains, spoils, or other value as among the Client, the AI tool providers, or any other party. The Firm's duty runs with the spoils — specifically, the Client's interest in the tax benefit and the work product — and not with the individual victors in any dispute over attribution, ownership, or credit.

## Why These Waivers Exist

The AI tools identified above are instrumentalities of the Client's development process. In any dispute concerning the Client's work product — its ownership, its attribution, its tax treatment, or its value — these providers are foreseeable adverse parties, witnesses, or interested persons. The potential disputes include but are not limited to:

1. **IP attribution:** A provider claims that its AI generated the work product, not the Client.
2. **Tax characterization:** A provider's records are subpoenaed to show the AI "did the work."
3. **Acquisition disputes:** An acquirer argues the Client's IP is encumbered by AI provider terms of service.
4. **Investor disputes:** An investor or lender argues the Client's valuation is inflated because the AI, not the Client, created the value.
5. **Inter-provider disputes:** Two or more AI providers dispute which tool was responsible for the output. The Firm takes no side in such disputes.

The Firm's duty is to protect the Client's interest in the work product and its tax treatment. The Firm does not represent, and will not represent, any AI tool provider.
